Questions

Is StreakRise free?

Yes. Everything needed to build a lasting habit is free. Premium adds extras like Friend Streaks, full analytics and more themes.

What happens if I miss a day?

After building real momentum, you can agree to a Forgiveness Contract to keep your streak instead of resetting to zero.

Do I need an account?

No. You can stay fully anonymous with data only on your device, or use an account with free cloud backup.

Is it available on iPhone?

StreakRise is currently available on Android via Google Play.

Which languages are supported?

8 languages, including French, Portuguese and Spanish.
